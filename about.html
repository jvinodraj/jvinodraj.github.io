About

Data engineer, now building for AI.

I’m a Data Engineer with 17+ years of experience, based in Chennai and working at Atos (Eviden).

My career started in embedded and database work in 2008. Since 2012 I have worked across client engagements in retail, payments and financial services, building and supporting production data platforms: pipelines, SQL and PL/SQL, Airflow, and the monitoring that keeps them reliable.

For the last year I have focused on R&D in Data Observability, Agentic AI and Business Intelligence modernization, including an MCP server that lets AI agents work with enterprise Power BI assets, and Dynatrace-based tracing for agentic applications.

Outside work I run in Chennai and analyze my own Garmin data. Those write-ups are on the blog.
